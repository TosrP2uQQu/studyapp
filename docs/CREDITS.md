# CREDITS.md — content licensing

Every bundled starter deck (`client/src/data/starter-decks.json`)
was written for this app from general knowledge. Nothing was copied
from Quizlet/Anki shared decks, paid courses, textbooks, or video
transcripts.

## Per-deck sources

- Lithuanian A1 phrases, Lithuanian grammar (7 cases),
  Lithuania basics: standard Lithuanian and civic facts
  (general knowledge, no third-party source).
- Algebra & geometry, Physics units & constants, Chemistry
  first 20 elements, English irregular verbs: standard school
  knowledge (public domain facts, own wording).
- Study science: paraphrased from Dunlosky et al. (2013),
  "Strengthening the student toolbox" (open-access review).
  No passages copied.

## Live third-party content (user-initiated only)

- Wikipedia extracts via the MediaWiki API shown with title,
  link, and CC BY-SA attribution in the UI.
- YouTube: search links and click-to-load nocookie embeds only.
  No transcripts, no downloads.

## Rule

Anything added later from the internet must be openly licensed
(CC BY-SA, CC BY, public domain), rewritten and verified, with
attribution recorded here and in the deck description.
